Who this class is for

Beginners, job seekers and career changers who need the computer skills employers expect: documents, spreadsheets, email and online applications.

What a session looks like

Eight weekly sessions, 2 hours each, 16 hours in total. You finish with a reviewed resume and practice on a video interview.

What to bring

A laptop if you have one. If you need a device, one is provided during sessions.

Online or onsite?

Both. Classes run online and onsite in the Portland metro area, with six learners at most and a human instructor beside you. Classes on the northern Oregon Coast can be planned with advance notice.

What does it cost?

Pricing is being finalized for our launch cohort. Join the interest list for founding-member rates. The free consultation (about 20 minutes, in person or by phone) and the Saturday intro workshop cost nothing.
